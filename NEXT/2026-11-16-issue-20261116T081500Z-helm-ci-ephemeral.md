title: Route helm-ci off the persistent pool
---

Public `helm-ci` lint-template now selects the untrusted lane with a hosted
tail and ignores `inputs.runner` on public Verjson repositories. Its
relay-backlog row leaves with that change. Part of #1831.

The job drops the stamped `caller-runner` marker so the shared catalog shape
(and Node CI) stay untouched. Private Verjson keeps the trusted lane;
non-Verjson callers stay on hosted. ADR 0235 records the route and the
backlog departure. `release.yml` `verify`'s pending digest is refreshed
because that row binds this checker; the job itself is unchanged.
