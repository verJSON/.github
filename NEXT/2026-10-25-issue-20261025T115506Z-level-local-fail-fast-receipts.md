Part of #1869. Typed Node CI levels carry strict, level-local failFast through generated callers, authorization, and both executors; omission defaults to true. Ordinary credentialed execution rejects false before candidate-controlled commands. Under false, an ordinary candidate nonzero exit is recorded, later entries run diagnostics, and the level remains failed. Under true, later entries receive not-run-fail-fast receipts. Candidate signals, ambiguous 128–255 exits, startup or confinement failures, cancellation, cleanup failures, and missing or malformed supervisor evidence stop the plan.

A trusted PID 1 supervisor records versioned per-entry and plan receipts beneath a fresh private host RUNNER_TEMP directory. Receipt identity includes repository, event, ref, executed head SHA, workflow SHA, run and attempt, job and level, full expanded-plan digest, ordinal, and entry identity. Candidate outcome, host cancellation signal, supervisor signal, and cleanup status are separate fields. Host handlers only enqueue signals; the supervisor latches cancellation, validates receipts, takes a final queue cutoff, and atomically publishes plan acceptance. If cancellation wins, completed entry outcomes remain factual while the plan is marked interrupted and cannot imply success.

PID 1 waits for the direct candidate, performs bounded namespace cleanup, and reaps descendants before another entry can launch. The candidate cannot access or forge the trusted status channel. Source and protected tests exercise local Bubblewrap paths, candidate self-signals, host cancellation during execution and receipt finalization, cleanup failures, and delayed descendant markers. Local Bubblewrap verification used 0.11.1; GitHub-hosted Bubblewrap acceptance remains pending. No credential, permission, egress, service, cache, workspace, or matrix behavior changes.

The host and PID 1 install SIGINT/SIGTERM handlers while signals are blocked, then unblock them. PID 1 validates sandbox security and prepares the candidate environment before readiness; after host ACK, the final signal poll immediately before Popen is the launch commit. Cancellation during validation or before the commit prevents candidate start; a signal after the commit is forwarded to the live candidate. If cleanup fails after a candidate runs, the infrastructure receipt retains its actual start state, return code or signal, supervisor signal, and failed cleanup. Cleanup-verification exceptions record cleanup=unverified and preserve known outcome and cause.

An interrupted plan has at most one interrupted entry; it begins the terminal not-run-aborted suffix, and no candidate-terminal or infrastructure-failure entry can appear in that plan. Cancellation at final acceptance reclassifies any fail-fast skipped suffix to not-run-aborted; not-run-fail-fast remains reserved for entries skipped after ordinary candidate failure. Cleanup-failure infrastructure receipts retain host cancellation in hostCancellationSignal; candidate signal records only a candidate-originated signal, and supervisorSignal must match host provenance. An infrastructure plan may carry a host signal only when a matching infrastructure-failure entry with cleanup failed or unverified carries it. Prelaunch cancellation followed by cleanup failure retains host provenance without claiming a candidate started or produced an outcome. If pidfd signaling reports ESRCH, the host does not claim forwarding and drains actual terminal status.
